Tideway

Your symptoms, your patterns — on your phone only.

A symptom tracker for perimenopause and menopause. It records what actually happened, shows you the patterns, and never predicts a cycle it cannot know.

  • Your data never disappears. Stored on your device, with export whenever you want it.
  • See everything at a glance, on one calendar.
  • Log a day in one tap. No opening each day one at a time.
  • The core stays free. All logging, all history, all patterns.
  • Pay once, or not at all. No subscription.

Reading material in the app summarises and links to the NHS and The Menopause Society. Tideway records what you tell it — it does not diagnose, and it never recommends a treatment or a dose.
