Tideway — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 September 2026

The short version

Everything you log in Tideway stays on your phone. Your symptoms, periods, medication and notes are stored in a database on your device. They are not uploaded, not synced, not backed up to us, and not shared with anyone. We cannot see them, because they never reach us.

There is one exception, and it is not health data: if you buy the report unlock, Google and our payments provider process that purchase. That is explained in full below.

We do not use analytics. We do not use advertising. We do not track you.

Who we are

Tideway is developed by Max (idwapro), an individual developer based in Kenya. For data-protection purposes, the developer is the data controller for the limited purchase information described below. We are not a controller of your health information, because we never receive it.

Contact: tideway.app@gmail.com

What Tideway stores on your device

Held locally, in an app database only Tideway can read:

  • symptoms you log, with severity and dates
  • lifestyle factors you log (sugar, alcohol, caffeine, stress and so on)
  • periods and bleeding you record
  • medication you add, and doses you mark as taken
  • sleep times you record
  • your app settings, such as text size

This information never leaves your device unless you deliberately export it (see *Backups*). Uninstalling Tideway deletes all of it permanently.

What we do not do

  • We do not upload your health information anywhere.
  • We do not have accounts, logins, or user profiles.
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • We do not show adverts, and we do not sell or share data with advertisers.
  • We do not use your data to train anything.
  • We have no server that stores your health information, so there is nothing about you for us — or anyone who compromised us — to disclose.

Purchases — the one exception

Tideway is free. Your first doctor-visit report is free. Unlimited reports can be unlocked with a one-time payment. There is no subscription.

If you make that purchase:

  • Google Play processes the payment. We never see your card or payment details. Google's handling is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat, our purchase-management provider, processes purchase history and an anonymous app user identifier so the app knows the unlock belongs to you and can restore it if you reinstall. RevenueCat acts as our processor. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

No health information is sent to Google or RevenueCat. They receive that a purchase happened — never what you logged.

If you never purchase, no personal data leaves your device at all.

Backups you create

You can export your data to a file. That file contains your health information in readable form.

Once you export it, it is yours and it is your responsibility — if you email it, save it to a cloud drive, or send it to someone, it is subject to that service's handling, not ours. We never receive it.

Because Tideway stores data only on your device, an export is the only way to move your history to a new phone or recover it if your device is lost. We would rather say that plainly than have you find out at the wrong moment.

The doctor report

Reports are generated on your device. Sharing or printing one is an action you take; the report goes wherever you send it. We never receive a copy.

Links to other websites

The reading section links to the NHS and The Menopause Society. Those are independent websites with their own privacy policies, and we have no control over them. We link to their material rather than copying it; following a link is your choice.

Children

Tideway is not intended for children and is not directed at anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

Because your health information stays on your device, you already have complete control of it: you can edit or delete any entry in the app, export it, or delete all of it by uninstalling.

For the purchase information described above, and depending on where you live — including under the UK GDPR and EU GDPR — you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your personal data, and to lodge a complaint with your data-protection authority. To exercise any of these, contact us at the address above. Purchase records can also be requested from or deleted via Google Play and RevenueCat.

Security

Your data is protected by your device's own security — your screen lock and Android's app sandbox, which prevents other apps from reading Tideway's database. Because nothing is transmitted, there is no data in transit to intercept and no account of yours for us to lose.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top and publish the new version at this address. If a future version of Tideway ever collects anything it does not collect today, we will say so clearly in the app before it happens — not quietly in a document.

Not medical advice

Tideway records what you tell it. It does not diagnose, and it does not recommend treatments or doses. Information in the reading section summarises and links to the NHS and The Menopause Society; decisions about your health belong with you and a qualified clinician.
